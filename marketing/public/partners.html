What you get

Income every month, and the repair work

A share of every monthly fee

FloorBeat is a monthly service. For every factory you bring in, you keep a share of its fee for as long as it stays with us. The share is agreed in writing before your first factory.

First call on repair work

FloorBeat records why every loom stops. When looms in a factory you brought keep stopping for mechanical or electrical faults, we send the factory to you first.

Trained to fit it

We show you how the sensors clamp on and how a board is mapped to its looms, so you can fit them yourself and get paid for it, or leave the fitting to us.

How it works

Three steps, no stock to hold

  1. 1

    Introduce a factory

    Tell us the mill and the owner. Open the ROI calculator with them on your phone: their looms, their stop hours, their rupees.

  2. 2

    We fit the pilot together

    Every loom in the pilot is live within the first week, and the owner sees running and stopped looms on their phone. We record you as the partner who brought the factory in.

  3. 3

    Paid every month

    When the factory signs up for the service, your share starts and comes every month the factory stays. No hardware to buy or stock: the sensors and boards are ours.
