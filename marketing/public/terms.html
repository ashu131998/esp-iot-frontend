In short

  • FloorBeat is for businesses. These terms and your Order (the quotation or invoice we agree with you) make up our agreement.

1. These terms

These terms are an agreement between FloorBeat ("we", "us"), based in Jaipur, Rajasthan, India, and the business that orders the service ("you"). They apply together with your Order: the quotation, order form or invoice that sets out your machines, fees and dates. Where the Order and these terms differ, the Order wins.

The person who accepts them confirms that they may do so for the business. The service is for businesses, not for personal use.

2. The service

FloorBeat is Machine Monitoring as a Service. For the machines in your Order we provide:

  • clamp-on current sensors and FloorBeat boards that measure the machines, online over Wi-Fi or 4G;
  • fitting, mapping each board to its machines, and calibrating running, idle and off for each machine;
  • your factory's own dashboard at its own address, with live status, history, alerts, shift reports and the monthly Results email;
  • support from our team.

We keep improving the service and may change how it looks or works, without taking away what your Order includes.

3. Pilots

A pilot is a trial on a few machines for an agreed time, and these terms apply to it. A free pilot has no fees, and either of us may end it at any time.

4. Boards and fitting

  • The boards and sensors remain FloorBeat's property. You pay for the service, not for the hardware.
  • We, or a partner we trust, fit them in your electrical panels following electrical safety practice. You give us safe access, any permit your site needs, and power and a network connection for the boards.
  • The boards measure your machines. They do not control or switch them.
  • Keep them where we fitted them, and do not open, move or change them. If one fails, we replace it free. If one is lost, or damaged by anything other than normal use, we may charge for its replacement.
  • Boards update their own software, and install only updates signed by FloorBeat.
  • When the subscription ends, give us access within 30 days to remove them.

5. Accounts

  • Your administrators create your accounts and decide what each person can see. You are responsible for what your accounts do, and for keeping their passwords secret.
  • An account can be signed in on at most two devices at a time. Signing in on a third ends the one used longest ago.
  • Tell us at once at support@floorbeat.in if you think an account has been misused.

6. Your data

  • Your factory's data is yours: its machine data and the details you enter about your people. You let us use it only to provide, support and improve the service for you, as our Privacy Policy describes.
  • We may use figures from which no customer or person can be identified, such as typical running hours by machine type, to improve the service.
  • For your people's details, you are the Data Fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and we process them on your behalf. You make sure you may give them to us: you tell your people and, where the law requires, ask their consent.
  • You can export your data as CSV from the dashboard at any time.

7. Fees and payment

  • You pay one monthly fee per monitored machine, as your Order sets out. Fees do not include GST, which we add to invoices.
  • Invoices are due within 15 days, unless your Order says otherwise.
  • If an invoice is still unpaid 15 days after we remind you, we may pause the service until it is paid. Your data stays safe meanwhile.
  • When a subscription period ends without being renewed, the dashboard shows data only up to its end date.
  • We may change fees by telling you at least 30 days before. A change applies from your next month.

8. Fair use

You must not:

  • try to reach other customers' data, or any part of our systems beyond your own access;
  • probe, attack or overload the service;
  • copy, reverse-engineer or resell the dashboard, the board software or the boards;
  • share logins publicly;
  • use the service against the law.

9. Availability and support

We run the service around the clock and watch it continuously, but we cannot promise it will never be interrupted: power cuts, network problems at the factory and planned maintenance can pause data or alerts. Boards keep their readings while their network is down and send them when it is back.

For help, write to support@floorbeat.in.

10. Not a safety system

FloorBeat helps you run production. Its alerts and figures can arrive late or not at all, for example during a power cut. Do not rely on them to protect people or equipment, or as your only record for legal or statutory purposes.

11. Confidentiality

Each of us keeps the other's non-public information confidential and uses it only for this agreement. This does not cover information that is public, that the other already knew, or that the law requires to be disclosed.

12. Who owns what

FloorBeat owns the service, the dashboard, the board designs and software, and everything else we build for it. You may use the service while your subscription lasts. If you send us ideas, we may use them freely.

13. Ending the subscription

  • The subscription runs month by month, unless your Order says otherwise. Either of us can end it at the end of any month by telling the other in writing at least 15 days before. Email is enough.
  • Either of us can end it at once if the other seriously breaks these terms and does not put it right within 15 days of being told.
  • After it ends, you can ask us for an export of your data for 90 days. Then we delete it, as our Privacy Policy describes. Fees already due remain payable.

  • Our total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees you paid in the 12 months before the claim.
  • None of this limits fees you owe us, your responsibility for our boards, or any liability the law does not allow to be limited, such as for fraud.

15. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms. We will email your administrators at least 30 days before a change takes effect. If you do not accept it, you can end the subscription before then.

16. Law and disputes

These terms are governed by the laws of India. We will first try to settle any dispute by talking. If that has not worked within 30 days, the courts at Jaipur, Rajasthan have exclusive jurisdiction.

17. General

  • These terms and your Order are the whole agreement between us.

  • Notices are given by email, to the addresses in your Order.
