The problem

Your machines are idle more than you think.

Most factories still rely on supervisors' notes and end-of-day counts. Short stops, slow restarts and silent breakdowns never make it onto paper — so the real cost of downtime stays invisible.

How it works

From clamp to dashboard in three steps

  1. 1

    Clip

    A current sensor clamps around the machine's power cable inside the panel. Nothing is cut, nothing is reprogrammed. One FloorBeat node on a DIN rail covers several machines.

  2. 2

    Connect

    The node detects when each machine starts and stops, and sends it to the FloorBeat cloud over Wi-Fi or 4G — every few seconds.

  3. 3

    See

    Owners, managers and supervisors see live status, availability and downtime on any phone or laptop — and get alerts and shift reports without asking anyone.
