How it works

From clamp to dashboard in three steps

  1. STEP 01

    Clip

    A current sensor clamps around the machine's power cable inside the panel. Nothing is cut, nothing is reprogrammed. One FloorBeat node on a DIN rail covers several machines.

  2. STEP 02

    Connect

    The node detects when each machine starts and stops, and sends it to the FloorBeat cloud over Wi-Fi or 4G, every few seconds.

  3. STEP 03

    See

    Owners, managers and supervisors see live status, availability and downtime on any phone or laptop, and get alerts and shift reports without asking anyone.

The service

One monthly fee per machine. Everything in it.

FloorBeat is Machine Monitoring as a Service: you do not buy hardware or software, you pay for monitored machines, month by month.

Sensors and boards

Clamp-on current sensors and an industrial board for every few machines. They stay ours: a faulty board is replaced, not billed.

Fitting and calibration

We fit them, map every board to its machines and calibrate running, idle and off for each machine type.

Dashboard and phone alerts

Live status, availability and the stop timeline for owners and managers. Stop alerts go to the operator on the machine.

Reports that prove it

A PDF after every shift, and every month the machine-hours won back against your first month, in hours and rupees.
