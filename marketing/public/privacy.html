In short

  • FloorBeat monitors machines, not people. Most of what our boards send is current, counts, temperature and vibration.
  • A factory's data belongs to that factory. We use it only to run FloorBeat for it.
  • We never sell personal data, we show no adverts, and floorbeat.in has no tracking or analytics.
  • Our servers are in Germany and our backups are encrypted.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time: write to support@floorbeat.in.

1. Who we are

FloorBeat ("we", "us") is based in Jaipur, Rajasthan, India.

This policy covers floorbeat.in, each factory's dashboard at its own address (for example factory-01.floorbeat.in), the alert app we provide, our staff consoles and the FloorBeat boards fitted in factories (together, "the service").

It follows the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the rules made under it ("DPDP Act"), and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules on reasonable security practices.

2. Whose data it is

FloorBeat handles personal data in two roles.

  • For a factory's own people. When a factory (our customer) enters its workers, the people who receive alerts and reports, or the accounts it creates, the factory decides why and how that data is used. The factory is the Data Fiduciary; FloorBeat processes the data on its behalf, only to provide the service. If you work at a factory that uses FloorBeat, ask your employer about your data first. We help them answer, and pass on any request you send to us.
  • For our own dealings. FloorBeat is the Data Fiduciary for the contact details of the people we deal with at customers and partners, for how accounts sign in and stay secure, for emails sent to us, and for visits to floorbeat.in.

4. Why we use it

  • To run the service: show each machine's status, send alerts, shift reports and the monthly Results email, and keep the boards connected and up to date.
  • To keep accounts safe: sign-in, password resets, limiting each account to two devices at a time, blocking password guessing, and recording what FloorBeat staff change.
  • To help you: answer questions, look into faults, and fix and improve the service.
  • To work with you as a customer: subscriptions, renewal reminders and invoices.
  • To meet the law: tax records, and requests from authorities that the law requires us to answer.

We use personal data only on a basis the DPDP Act allows: your consent, or a legitimate use it permits, such as data given to us voluntarily for a specific purpose, or a legal obligation. For a factory's own people, the factory is responsible for telling them and, where needed, asking their consent.

5. What we never do

  • We never sell or rent personal data.
  • We never use it for advertising, and we show no adverts.
  • We never use one factory's data for another customer.
  • We never send marketing to a factory's workers.
  • floorbeat.in has no analytics, tracking or advertising cookies.

7. Where it is stored

Our servers and database are in Germany, with Hetzner. Our backups are stored encrypted with Cloudflare. Some of the companies above work from other countries, including the United States. The DPDP Act allows this except to countries the Government of India restricts, and we will follow any such restriction.

9. How we protect it

  • Every connection is encrypted. Boards connect to our servers over encrypted links that check our certificate.
  • Passwords are kept as one-way hashes, sign-in tokens are short-lived, and an account can be signed in on at most two devices at a time.
  • Each factory sees only its own data, at its own address, and each person sees only what their role allows.
  • Boards install only software updates signed by FloorBeat: each board checks the signature first.
  • Only authorised FloorBeat staff can reach our servers, and what staff change in a factory's settings is recorded.
  • Backups are encrypted, and the service is watched around the clock.

If a breach affects personal data, we will tell the affected customers and people, and the Data Protection Board of India, as the law requires.

10. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can:

  • get a summary of the personal data we hold about you, how we use it, and who we shared it with;
  • have it corrected, completed or updated;
  • have it erased once it is no longer needed, or when you withdraw your consent, unless the law requires us to keep it;
  • withdraw a consent you gave, as easily as you gave it (this does not affect what was done before);
  • name someone to use these rights for you if you die or cannot act yourself;
  • have your complaint answered.

Write to support@floorbeat.in, to the Grievance Officer named below. We answer within 30 days. If your data was entered by your employer, we pass your request to them or act on their instructions. If our answer does not settle it, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
