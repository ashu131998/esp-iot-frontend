FloorBeat Alerts, on your phone

The minute a machine stops, the people on it know — and tell you why with one tap. Sign in with the email and password your factory admin gave you on the Team page.

Android

Download for Android (APK)

  1. Download the file above and open it. If the phone asks, allow installs from your browser for this one app.
  2. Open FloorBeat Alerts, sign in with your work email and password.
  3. Turn on notifications when asked, and keep the app on the phone (do not swipe it away from recent apps).

No Google account needed. A Play Store listing follows; the app updates itself as the service does.

iPhone

  1. Open alerts.floorbeat.in in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open FloorBeat Alerts from the Home Screen, sign in, and turn notifications on in its settings.

iPhones only ring for apps on the Home Screen (iOS 16.4 or newer): the step above is what makes the alerts arrive.

What it does

  • Machine stopped: the operator on it is asked why — one tap, and the dashboard shows the reason.
  • Running again: which configuration is on, or who got it going.
  • Shift reminders: before the shift, with the machines and the task.
  • Owners and supervisors: every stop and restart, with who was asked.

English, Hindi and Gujarati. Alerts stay in the app's inbox until read, whether or not the phone rang.

Not getting alerts?

  • Open the app once and check Settings → Notifications says they are on; use Send a test.
  • Battery saver and "optimise battery" settings can delay alerts: allow FloorBeat Alerts to run in the background.
  • Forgot the password? Your factory admin sets a new one on the dashboard's Team page.
  • Still stuck: support@floorbeat.in.
