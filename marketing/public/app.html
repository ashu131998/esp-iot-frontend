FloorBeat, on your phone

One app for your factory: its alerts, its dashboard and its stock. Open it and pick one. Sign in with the email and password your factory admin gave you on the Team page.

Android

Download FloorBeat for Android (APK)

  1. Download the file above and open it. If the phone asks, allow installs from your browser for this one app.
  2. Open FloorBeat, sign in with your work email and password.
  3. Turn on notifications when asked, and keep the app on the phone (do not swipe it away from recent apps).

Have FloorBeat Alerts already? This is its new version: it installs over it, and alerts keep ringing. No Google account needed. A Play Store listing follows; the app updates itself as the service does.

iPhone

  1. Open dashboard.floorbeat.in in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open FloorBeat from the Home Screen, sign in, and turn notifications on when it asks.

iPhones only ring for apps on the Home Screen (iOS 16.4 or newer): the step above is what makes the alerts arrive. FloorBeat Alerts, if it is on the phone already, goes on ringing too.

What is in it

  • Alerts. Machine stopped: the operator on it is asked why — one tap, and the dashboard shows the reason. Running again: which configuration is on, or who got it going. Shift reminders: before the shift, with the machines and the task. Owners and supervisors: every stop and restart, with who was asked.
  • Dashboard. Which machines are running, what stopped and for how long, shift by shift.
  • Stock. Add stock and see what is on the shelf, where your factory uses it.

English, Hindi and Gujarati. Alerts stay in the app's inbox until read, whether or not the phone rang. The same app opens on a computer at dashboard.floorbeat.in.

Not getting alerts?

  • Open the app, tap Alerts, then Settings: check Notifications says they are on; use Send a test.
  • Battery saver and "optimise battery" settings can delay alerts: allow FloorBeat to run in the background.
  • Forgot the password? Your factory admin sets a new one on the dashboard's Team page.
  • Still stuck: support@floorbeat.in.
