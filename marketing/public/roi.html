How FloorBeat measures it

  • Your first month of monitoring is the baseline: each machine's own running time, measured by its sensor.
  • Every month after, the monthly results report states the machine-hours won back against it, and what they are worth at your rupees per machine-hour.
  • Shifts when the whole floor was closed (holidays, Sundays, a power cut) are left out of both, so they never count as time lost or won.
  • The same report counts the stop alerts your team acted on and how fast machines ran again.
